🎵 Quizrine Audio Debug Tool

Use this page to reset audio settings if the player isn't working correctly.

This will clear all saved audio preferences and let the app start fresh with default settings.

Current Settings:

Audio Enabled: checking...

Shuffle Enabled: checking...

Volume: checking...

What these buttons do:

  • Clear Audio Settings: Removes all saved audio preferences
  • Force Enable Audio: Turns on the audio system
  • Force Enable Shuffle: Turns on shuffle mode
  • Refresh Status: Shows current saved settings

After clicking any button, go back to your quiz app and refresh the page.

Manual Console Commands:

You can also open the browser console (F12) and run:

  • localStorage.removeItem('quizrine_audio_enabled')
  • localStorage.removeItem('quizrine_audio_shuffle')
  • localStorage.removeItem('quizrine_audio_volume')
